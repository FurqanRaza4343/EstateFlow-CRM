Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

At EstateFlow CRM, we protect administrative listings and customer interactions using advanced cryptographic measures. This document outlines how we collect, process, and safeguard your data.

1. Information We Collect

We only ingest data essential to delivering high-efficiency real-estate workflows:

  • Account Credentials: Full name, verified business email, telephone numbers.
  • Lead Registry Data: Client budgets, preferred property locations, deal records, lead temperature scores.
  • Property Listings: Property addresses, pricing, photographic metadata, listing statuses.
  • Operational Records: Interaction times, call logs, SMS logs, login timelines.
  • Billing Info: Subscription billing details, seat numbers, white-label settings.

2. Purpose & Use

All data is used strictly for executing core business logistics: CRM optimization, call logging, custom branding, and system security.

3. Third-Party Integrations

We do not sell agency customer listings. Data is shared only with:

  • InsForge — database and edge function hosting
  • Clerk — authentication and user management

4. Your Rights

You may request data export, correction, or permanent deletion at any time via in-app account settings or by contacting support.

5. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact your agency administrator or EstateFlow support through the app.
